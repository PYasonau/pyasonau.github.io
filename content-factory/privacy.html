DreamPY Content Factory

Privacy policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy covers DreamPY Content Factory, the owner-operated publishing tool, and its informational website. It does not replace the separate privacy policies of our mobile games.

1. Operator and contact

The operator and controller for information processed by the tool is Pavel Yasonau, Poland. Contact: dream.quiz.support@gmail.com. DreamPY is the name used for this app and content-publishing project.

2. Intended use

Content Factory runs locally for the operator’s own authorized publishing accounts. There is no public account registration or hosted account-connection service on this website.

3. Information accessed

  • Google and YouTube: OAuth access and refresh tokens; connected channel ID and title; the operator-selected video files, titles, descriptions and publication settings; uploaded video IDs, processing state and visibility. The tool uses the YouTube upload and read-only scopes. Read-only authorization can technically permit broader YouTube account access; the implemented workflow reads channel identity and video processing/publication status.
  • Facebook and Instagram: authorized Facebook Page and linked professional Instagram account IDs and username, user and Page access tokens, granted permissions, token validity and expiry information, publishing quota, upload/container IDs, captions, processing state and published media links. Requested permissions are instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list and pages_read_engagement, together with the platform’s basic profile permission.
  • Operational records: approved content snapshots, schedules, checksums, upload session URLs, delivery states, timestamps and redacted error codes.
  • Support: email address, correspondence and attachments that you choose to send.

The implemented workflow does not request Gmail messages, Google Drive files, social-media passwords, direct messages or advertising-management access.

4. Use and sharing

Information is used to authorize the correct account, upload and publish approved content, avoid accidental duplicates, check delivery and diagnose failures. Content and the credentials necessary to act on it are transmitted to the relevant Google/YouTube or Meta API. Approved videos and captions become visible to the audience selected for publication.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising profiles, or used to train AI models. The tool does not use Google account data to generate advertising audiences or provide it to data brokers. Human access is limited to the operator and authorized maintenance needed for this workflow, subject to applicable platform rules. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to address security incidents.

DreamPY Content Factory’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Storage and security

Credentials and delivery records are stored on the operator’s computer. Credential files are restricted to the local owner and excluded from source control. Upload session URLs are treated as credentials. This public GitHub Pages site contains no account tokens or delivery database. Local file permissions do not by themselves provide encryption or protection against a compromised device.

Records are kept while needed for account connection, pending delivery, troubleshooting and the operator’s publication history. Cleanup is currently managed by the operator; there is no automatic fixed retention period for every local record. Data that is no longer needed should be removed. Applicable legal requirements may require particular records to be retained longer.

6. Disconnecting, deletion and choices

The account owner can stop the worker, cancel eligible pending jobs and remove the local credentials and records. Google authorization can be revoked through Google Account connections. Meta access can be reviewed or removed in Facebook’s Apps and Websites or Business Integrations settings. Revocation prevents future authorized API calls but does not automatically remove local copies.

For deletion requests, email dream.quiz.support@gmail.com. The operator will verify the request and remove relevant local credentials and records within 30 days, unless a lawful retention requirement applies. Deleting a local job does not delete an already published YouTube video or Instagram Reel; those can be managed through the respective platform.

7. Website and service providers

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes visitor technical information, including IP addresses for security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising cookies, forms or third-party fonts to these pages. Email correspondence is handled through the operator’s email provider.

Google/YouTube, Meta, GitHub and the email provider process information under their own policies and may process it internationally, including outside Poland or the European Economic Area. See the Google Privacy Policy and Meta Privacy Policy for their practices.

8. Rights and updates

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or withdraw consent for future access. Processing for the owner-operated workflow relies on the relevant authorization and legitimate interests in publishing and securing the operator’s content; support may also be needed to fulfil a request or legal obligation. Contact the operator to exercise your rights. You may complain to the competent supervisory authority, including Poland’s UODO.

The publishing tool is not offered to children. Material changes to the tool’s handling of account data will be reflected in this policy and, where required, in renewed account authorization. The effective date identifies this version.
