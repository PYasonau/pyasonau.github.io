YOUTUBE

Upload and verify.

Uses YouTube API Services to upload approved videos to the connected channel and check channel identity, processing and visibility. Google authorization requests upload and read-only YouTube access.

INSTAGRAM

Publish approved Reels.

Uses Meta’s Instagram API with Facebook Login to identify the connected professional account, transfer a local video and publish its approved caption.

For our own publishing accounts.

This is a local tool used by the operator for DreamPY content. This website describes it; it does not accept account connections, upload files or collect social-media passwords.

Third-party use is not currently offered. Availability and account access depend on the relevant platform.
