Slimezylab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Slimezylab (com.kamsonau.monsterlab) is an offline game. It does not ask you to create an account.

Information used by the game

The game saves your unlocked experiments, best star results, earned star balance, and cosmetic choices in a file on your device. This information is used to restore your progress and purchases made with stars earned in the game. The current Android version does not send this information to us or to a third party. It has no advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or in-app payment SDK.

The game does not request Android permissions or access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or health information. It does not have chat or user-generated content features.

Support email

If you choose to email dream.quiz.support@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you put in your message so that we can reply. Email is handled by the email provider you use and by Google, which hosts our support mailbox. Please do not send sensitive personal information. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence by writing to the same address. We keep support messages only as long as needed to answer or follow up on your request, subject to any legal obligation to retain them.

Storage and deletion

Game progress remains on your device until you clear Slimezylab's app storage or uninstall the app. The game does not currently have an in-app progress reset button. It does not create an online account, so there is no account to delete. If you email support, you may request deletion of that correspondence as described above.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the game's data practices change. The updated date above will change as well. For privacy questions, email dream.quiz.support@gmail.com.
