Slimezylab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Slimezylab (com.kamsonau.monsterlab) is a game for players aged 13 and over. You can play without creating an account or making a payment. Contact: dream.quiz.support@gmail.com.

Game progress

The game saves unlocked experiments, best star results, earned stars, and cosmetic choices on your device. We use this information to restore your progress. It is not uploaded to our server. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. There is no online account to delete.

Advertising and consent

The Android version includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show an occasional full-screen ad between completed experiments. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks whether a privacy choice is required in your region and may show a consent form before the game requests ads. If the consent check fails or a required choice has not been completed, the game continues without requesting ads. Choosing not to consent to personalized ads does not prevent you from playing.

When the advertising SDK runs, Google may receive your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app interactions such as launches and ad views, diagnostic and performance information, and device or advertising identifiers. Google uses these data for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. The SDK encrypts data in transit. The game does not send your saved progress or star balance to Google. Google may retain advertising data under its own policies; we do not have a copy of those SDK records to delete. See Google's privacy policy and Google's ad controls for more information and choices. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in your device settings.

The game has no separate analytics, crash reporting, subscription, or in-app payment SDK. Its cosmetic items are obtained with stars earned in the game.

Permissions and other information

The Android ad SDK uses network access and may use the advertising ID. Slimezylab does not ask for precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or health data. There is no chat or user-generated content.

If you email support, we receive your email address and anything you include in the message to answer your request. Google hosts our support mailbox. Please do not send sensitive personal information. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing dream.quiz.support@gmail.com; we keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, subject to any legal obligation to retain it.

Changes

We will update this policy if the game's data practices change. For privacy questions, email dream.quiz.support@gmail.com.
