Slimezylab Support

For help with Slimezylab, email dream.quiz.support@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS or Android version, game version, and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Slimezylab does not use player accounts. Progress and earned stars are stored on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes local progress; we cannot restore it from a server.

For questions about data handling, see the Privacy Policy.
